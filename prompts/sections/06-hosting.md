# Section: Hosting & Operations

> **Adapt to the project type.** For software: hosting, deployment, CI/CD.
> For physical businesses: location, distribution, logistics.

## What to Capture
- Where this operates (hosting platform, physical location, distribution channels)
- How changes are deployed or rolled out
- Environments or locations (dev/staging/prod, or pilot/launch locations)
- Domain, address, or access point
- Estimated monthly operational cost

## Questions to Ask

### For digital products:
1. Does this need a server or can it be static? (Huge cost difference)
2. Do you need staging/preview environments for testing?
3. What's your expected traffic? (10 users/day? 10,000?)
4. Do you have a domain name in mind?
5. How important is uptime?

### For physical businesses/services:
1. Where will this operate? (Fixed location, mobile, online, hybrid?)
2. How will customers find and access you?
3. What's the capacity? (Seats, customers/day, orders/hour?)
4. Do you need specific permits, licenses, or zoning?
5. What are the operating hours and staffing needs?

## Quality Tips
- Start small and scale. Don't over-commit on space, hosting, or capacity.
- For software: static sites are free and fast — use them when possible.
- For physical: location determines traffic — research foot traffic, competitors nearby.
- Always have a way to test changes before they affect real customers.
- Free tiers exist for many hosting platforms. Start there for digital products.

## Common Mistakes
- Paying for capacity before you have demand
- No way to test changes safely (deploying/launching untested)
- Not considering ongoing operational costs
- Choosing a complex setup when simple would work
- Ignoring how customers actually get to you
