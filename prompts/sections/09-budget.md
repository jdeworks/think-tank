# Section: Budget & Resources

## What to Capture
- Development effort (person-hours or person-weeks)
- Infrastructure/operations costs (hosting, rent, equipment, databases, CDN)
- Third-party/external costs (services, vendors, licenses, subscriptions)
- Total estimate (one-time + monthly recurring)

## Questions to Ask
1. Is this a solo project or do you have a team?
2. What's your monthly budget for running this? ($0? $50? $500?)
3. Are you doing the development yourself or hiring?
4. Any paid services you already use or plan to use?
5. What's the maximum you'd spend before deciding this isn't worth it?

## Quality Tips
- Always calculate Total Cost of Ownership: development + hosting + services + maintenance.
- Free tiers exist for almost everything. Start there.
- Development time IS a cost, even if you're not paying yourself.
- Plan for the cost of 10x growth — will your costs scale linearly or exponentially?
- Monitoring and logging are often forgotten costs but critical for production.

## Common Mistakes
- Not accounting for ongoing maintenance/operations time
- Forgetting recurring costs (domain renewal, subscriptions, supplies, rent)
- Choosing "enterprise" tools for a solo project
- Not setting a budget cap (spend $X/month max, then reassess)
- Ignoring the cost of your own time
