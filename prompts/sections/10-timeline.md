# Section: Timeline & Milestones

## What to Capture
- Phases (MVP, v1, v2, etc.) with duration and deliverables
- Milestones with target dates and descriptions
- Dependencies between phases

## Questions to Ask
1. When do you want this to be usable? (Even if basic)
2. What's the absolute minimum you need for a "version 1"?
3. Are there external deadlines? (Conference, launch event, school project?)
4. How many hours per week can you dedicate to this?
5. What features can wait for v2?

## Quality Tips
- Ship the smallest useful thing first. You can always add more.
- Double your time estimate. Software always takes longer than expected.
- Define "done" for each phase — vague milestones lead to never-ending development.
- MVP means Minimum VIABLE Product — it should solve the core problem, not be a prototype.
- Weekly milestones keep momentum. Monthly milestones lose focus.

## Common Mistakes
- No MVP — trying to build everything at once
- Unrealistic timelines (most people underestimate by 2-3x)
- Feature creep during development (adding "just one more thing")
- Not defining what "done" means for each milestone
- Planning too far ahead — detailed plans for 6+ months out are fiction
